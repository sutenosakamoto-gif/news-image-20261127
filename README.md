One broadsheet page summing up Friday 27 November 2026: the EU summit
ending with no deal on the budget or the Ukraine loan, a drone crossing
into Latvia, and China stocks jumping after Beijing vowed market
support — 37 stories in all, with a numbers strip, a UTC timeline, a
pinned world map and a DATE CHECK that names what was left out because
it belonged to another day.

Built at build time: `content.py` holds the day's verified stories,
`render.py` draws the page with PIL and real OFL fonts into `page.png`,
and `main.py` copies that finished image to `/output/entry.png`.
`STORYLIST.md` records the selection and `SOURCES.md` the sources.
